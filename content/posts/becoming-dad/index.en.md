*Austrian girl and her grandparents*

I knew I wanted to be a parent and have kids when I was in middle school. Back then I thought that life is quite amazing. It was the time of innocence and freedom from obligations. Just me and the boys (and the legos and video games). I didn't really know with whom I would have kids, or when. 

{{< center >}}...{{< /center >}}

Long ago, I was in Austria, in a cafe surrounded by people speaking strange words, not quite German. I ordered a coffee, and I took out my reMarkable 2 tablet and started to draw a little girl on it. I looked at her, and back to the e-ink. Her grandparents and mother were with her, and I drew the grandparents.

As the figures, dots and lines became more real, and a face started to vividly form, I became increasingly moved and sensitive. Her - the toddler's - cheeks were so bubbly and cute, the eyes so beautiful and her hair so pretty. The rain started to pour heavily, banging the metal roof. My mind felt open with expression. The abstraction washed away.

The rain penetrated the roof, into tears, and I knew, I really wanted a family. Back then I was still going through a breakup that really fractured my heart. I fractured *others'* hearts. 

I dried the tears quickly, as I didn't want anyone to see it, lest the toddler's family. 

{{< center >}}...{{< /center >}}

Once upon a time, something or someone asked me how come one plus one can equal three. Indeed, mathematically impossible, but in real life, everything is possible. Sometimes one plus one equals, three, at times four, and perhaps even five. That'd be a nightmare or a jackpot. 

You see, *when daddy and mommy love each other very much*, a baby is born. Now you see right? Hehe. I am going to be a dad! Surprise!(?) 

The moment we found out, I was giggling nonchalantly and I told Darlene in the morning that I made porridge for *three*. She blurted a sarcastic "haa-haa". 

Apparently Darlene's Rwandan mother living in Uganda knew that her daughter would get pregnant this year. She also says it is going to be a girl. Hmm. *Let's see.* ("Katsotaan")

{{< center >}}...{{< /center >}}

Fast forward in time, life goes so fast and I wasn't even able to write that I got engaged, let alone *even* married! Soon I'll be eighty and wrinkly for all I know. 

We got engaged last year in August, after my meditation retreat. We married the following year in April, in a quite ordinary court. Some of my friends cheered that idea.

But let's just say that the court-room-marriage-experience was quite "something". The Mrs Couldn't Care Less who wed us had obviously done this for the millionth time, and made me cringe so bad. The tension of wanting to be romantic and yet stay official because of the Law of Finland tome in front of me made me self-conscious of the situation. It felt so institutionally mundane and official. 

I am excitedly looking forward to celebrating it properly with friends and family, and unify us more ceremonially. Hopefully sooner than later.

{{< center >}}...{{< /center >}}

After meeting [God]({{< ref "posts/tears-of-god/index.md" >}}), it became obvious in the *experiential* level that things would move fast. I am here for the ride, to experience, observe and witness the pace of life. Hopefully, life takes a slower turn, when I can meditate more intentionally. Now it seems, things are about to *change*, and things require reshuffling priorities.  

And 'change' is the word here: how to remain peaceful, aware, and happy when things happen not only inside of me, but inside my wife, the baby, and the world around me? How do we keep calm with everything happening so fast? 

{{< center >}}...{{< /center >}}

I don't really know what I feel just yet. Things are still somewhat the same as they were: we live in the same place, work remains elusive, plans are being made, dreams crafted, and as my wife says: Ville, you are turning into a laptop. That is, I spend a lot of time behind the screen and keyboard. Honestly, there's a lot of pressure to land at some stable source of money. Then again, after all, all the baby needs is love and attention. Money is secondary. 

Previously, I wrote articles for [OpenSats](/posts/what-opensats-left-me-with), but that turned out to be short-lived. It motivated me to become an entrepreneur and take no shit from anyone anymore. Now I create work for myself, rather than chase after work and get rejected, or worse, laid off. 

So I decided to build a marketplace for firewood, [Klapii](https://klapii.com), where all firewood sellers can come around. Now the capitalistic, competition-hustle mindset is spreading in my mind, and I am not sure what I'll do with that. I have a real desire to create and build something tangible that is useful for others, and as such useful for me. Let's see. 

And yet, really, *really* what I want is a slower life. I know it'd do good for me, my wife and my baby. But right now, things are as they are, and I know I'll succeed. It just needs some time, effort, and perseverance. Luckily, I have an *incredibly* patient wife. Idk what I'd do without her? Hence I am not so concerned about the future because I know Darlene will be a great mother. It's nice to do life together. 

Be well.
