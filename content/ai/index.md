I use AI to code, research, brainstorm, assess, analyse, paraphrase, experiment, generate lists and tables, get feedback, structure notes, and chat with. 

I designed this blog with AI, my server, my newsletter, my [analytics](https://subscribe.kokkomaki.com/subscription/form), a website [debankd.org](https://debankd.org/), and much more. It is a phenomenal tool, and I find that I am able to express my creativity in ways that I could not have before. 

At the end of the day: first comes experience, then storytelling. If I am not able to tell the story in my own words, with the help of AI, I wasn't a great storyteller to begin with. Therefore, whatever you read here, is always accepted by me before anything gets published. 

*Declaration subject to change faster than the declaration of independence.*
